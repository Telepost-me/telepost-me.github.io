# Limits

This page contains known Telepost limits and restrictions (found empirically).

## Character limits in posts

Each post type may have different limits:

* Text: no more than 4000 characters
* Photo: no more than 1024 characters

## Data size in posts

Each post type may have different limits:

* Photo - an attempt to exceed the limit results in the [error "Check the entered data"](./errors/post-text-attach-error.mdx):
  * size - no more than 5 MB
  * resolution - no more than 5000 pixels wide or high

* Album - an attempt to exceed the limit results in the [error `Request Entity Too Large`](./errors/error-request-entity-too-large.mdx):
  * total size of all attached media files — no more than 50 MB

## Number of posts

No more than 500 posts per month.

An attempt to exceed the limit results in the error message (`Error 429`): `Limit Exceeded`. Too many posts created today, max: 500. Also applies to [repeat posts](./tutorials/repeat-post.mdx).

## Number of channels for publishing a post

No more than 5 channels for (simultaneous) posting.

If more than 5 are selected, the "Save" button becomes inactive. More details in [issue #22](https://github.com/Telepost-me/support/issues/22).

## Number of uploaded files

A maximum of 2000 files can be uploaded per day when creating posts.

An attempt to exceed the limit results in the error message (`Error 429`): `Limit Exceeded. Max files uploaded today: 2000`.

## Number of connected channels/chats

No more than 200 connected channels/chats per account.

An attempt to exceed the limit results in the error message: `Too many channels created. Maximum 200. More details in [issue #96](https://github.com/Telepost-me/support/issues/96).

---

Limits may change in the future, and in any direction.
